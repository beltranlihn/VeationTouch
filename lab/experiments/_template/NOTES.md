# Lab template — Halo

- **Status:** template
- **Kind:** visual
- **Author:** Alma Digital Studio
- **Updated:** 2026-11-23

## Concept
Every hand leaves a soft light that slowly dissolves on a dark, slowly breathing mist. The starting point to copy: it shows the
runtime calls, strokes, metres, seamless wrap, parameters and a feedback texture in one small file.

## Solo path
One visitor drags a hand along a wall: a long luminous line that fades in about 7 s.

## Group path
Up to 16 hands at once, each with its own tint; the lights merge where they cross.

## Controls
Glow size, Fade, Brightness, Ambience, Quality (see `manifest.json`).

## Audio
None. Pair it with a sound piece: `?sound=<id>` in the lab, or the Audio card in the app.

## Status and tests
Runs in the lab (`node lab/harness/serve.mjs`). Not tested in the app yet.
