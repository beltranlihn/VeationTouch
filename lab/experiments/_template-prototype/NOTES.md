# Prototype template — Blooms

- **Status:** template
- **Kind:** visual+sound
- **Author:** Alma Digital Studio
- **Updated:** 2026-11-23

## Concept
A standalone browser prototype in one file, with the lab's conventions: the mouse is a hand, Shift+click pins a hand, virtual
hands play at human speed when nobody touches. A tap blooms a soft light and strikes a bell on its own wall; a drag leaves a thread
of small blooms. It is the starting point for an idea you want to see and hear in an afternoon.

## Solo path
One hand: a tap, a bell, a slow thread when it moves.

## Group path
`G` plays 2, 4 or 8 virtual hands; Shift+click pins as many as you want.

## Controls
Click / drag, Shift+click (pin), F (room strip), B (body scale), H (help), S (sound), D (demo), G (group), R (remove pinned), L (LiDAR feel).

## Audio
Simulated 4.1 room (4 wall channels and a sub, meters bottom right). A bell per touch on the touched wall; pitch from the height in
metres on a pentatonic scale.

## Status and tests
Runs by double click or at `/experiments/_template-prototype/index.html` in the lab server.
