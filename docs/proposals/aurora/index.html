Twelve chord stones, three per wall, 1.1–1.4 m high (children reach them). The progression is a Dorian loop, one chord per wall: Dm9 (P4), Fmaj7 (P1), Cadd9 (P2), Gadd9 (P3). A solo visitor builds a chord by moving along a wall; walking quickly through three walls inside the fade window crowns the sky.
WebGL2 with float render targets is required.
