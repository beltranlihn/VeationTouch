Breathe in for 4 s, out for 6 s. Rest a hand on a stone for 0.6 s to wake it; it lives 8 s after the hand leaves. When every wall has an awake stone at the same time, the chord blooms and the room moves to the next light (dawn, day, dusk, night). Stones are 80 cm wide at 1.0 – 1.6 m. A woken stone glows ~30 s after you leave (less in a crowd), so one person can walk the four walls and bloom it alone. The image wraps (P3 continues into P4). With no input for 4 s the auto-demo plays.
WebGL2 is required.
