One person with two hands (up to 1.6 m apart) already makes a high, bell-like thread. Threads across walls (several people) are deeper, wider, and add a fifth. Length = pitch (3 octaves, just intonation, always consonant). A hand alone sings by its x position. Height sets brightness and decay up to 3.0 m; the image is 3.75 m. The room wraps horizontally: P3 continues into P4. With no input for 4 s the auto-demo plays.
WebGL2 with float render targets is required.
